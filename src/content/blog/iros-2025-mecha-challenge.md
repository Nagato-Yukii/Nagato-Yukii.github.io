At IROS 2025 in Hangzhou, I competed as part of Shanghai University's **Future Tense** team in the **MECHA Challenge (Robot Dance Championship)**.

The event focused on humanoid robot dance, including whole-body motion design and control, motion stability, and synchronization between movement and music.

Our team — Zhang Jinrong, Zhang Yulai, and Kaixuan Chen — received **third place**.
