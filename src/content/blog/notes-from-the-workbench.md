> Sample entry — its content does not describe real events.

Finished projects tend to look inevitable. The useful decisions, wrong turns, and small discoveries disappear behind the final result.

Workbench notes preserve some of that context. They do not need to become tutorials. A date, a question, a command, and one sentence about the outcome are often enough.

```text
2024.10.03 / question
What changed, and what did I learn from it?
```

Write close to the work. Edit later only when clarity needs it.
