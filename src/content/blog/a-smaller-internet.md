A personal page does not have to behave like a platform. It can simply be a stable address with a name on it.

The old web had many rough edges, but it also made room for distinct places. A narrow column, a few links, and a list of dated documents can still do useful work.

Small pages load quickly. They are easy to understand, easy to archive, and easy to rebuild. Their limits are part of their character.
